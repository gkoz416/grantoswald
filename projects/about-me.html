ENGR 110

Mousetrap Car Competition

Designed a mousetrap-powered car that was able to compete in various competitions: acceleration, speed, braking, and distance on a team of 4 students.

Coded a program in MATLAB to calculate a projected top speed and how far it was able to go based on inputted gear ratios.

The car was required to have some sort of "transmission," utilizing at least 2 gear ratios: one for speed and another for distance.
