Intro to Engineering Fundamentals

Mousetrap Car Competition

Project Goals: Design and build a car powered by a mousetrap spring, designing for both speed and distance by including a simple transmission in the plans.

Photo Gallery

CAD renders and a photo of the mousetrap car

Design process

01

Set Goals and Timeline

The team met and decided a rough outline of what the car should look like and what we wanted the car to do (e.g., top speed, maximum distance, braking distance, etc.

02

MATLAB Simulations

Once gear ratios, wheel sizes, and propulsion arm dimensions were established, we calculated what the top speed, maximum distance, and maximum acceleration would be using equations provided by our professor.

03

SolidWorks Modeling

Once the goals and dimensions were set, we modeled the car in CAD and determined how to manufacture each part. For this project, at least two different manufacturing processes were required.

04

Manufacturing and Testing

Lastly, the team used tools resources from the university's MakerSpace and 3D printing lab to bring the car to life. Once the car was put together, we were able to test the car with enough time to tweak the design as needed.

Lessons Learned & Challenges Overcome

  • Basic understanding of how to use SolidWorks and MATLAB
  • Time management to allow for unforeseen roadblocks (3D print lab's request log getting heavily backed up)
